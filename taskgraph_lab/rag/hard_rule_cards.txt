RULE: When the question asks where A is relative to B, LOCATE A, LOCATE B,
then use RELATION(A,B). RELATION returns the unknown relation. Do not replace it
with SELECT_REL.

RULE: For relational counting, LOCATE the candidates and reference, filter with
SELECT_REL(candidates,reference,known_relation), then make COUNT consume that
filtered result. Never count the original unfiltered candidates.

RULE: SELECT_REL filters candidates when the relation is stated in the question.
RELATION is used when the relation itself is the requested answer. Preserve
subject/reference direction.

RULE: Resolve nested referring expressions in dependency order. Attach each
inside, near, next-to, ordinal, rank, or region constraint to the entity phrase
it modifies. Every relation required by the question must reach the final
dependency chain.

RULE: ROUTE_PLANNING normally locates the start and goal, calls
BUILD_ROUTE_CONTEXT(image,start,goal), and uses FINAL_QUESTION. The Planner does
not solve the road path.
